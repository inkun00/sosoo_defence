MODIFICATIONS BY SOSOO DEFENCE
Silence trimming, light EQ, layer mixing for the weapons listed in the report,
short fades, finished-mix level balancing, mono downmix, 24kHz/64kbps MP3
encoding and removal of nonessential metadata. The original attack dynamics
are preserved without dynamic compression during asset preparation.
Only siege uses a 0.95 playback-rate adjustment; other clips retain the
original source pitch. Source filenames, hashes, timing and settings are in
licenses/tower-audio-v1.json. Reproduction: tools/prepare-tower-audio.py.

CC-BY source material and the corresponding derived clips retain the stated
attribution licenses. They are not offered under CC0. CC0 source material
remains available under CC0. For layered clips, retain every source credit
listed in the report when redistributing the audio.

Source archives are not included in the web release. The optimized audio is
loaded from this game's own assets/audio/towers/ directory. Music, UI,
collision and emergency fallback effects remain original local Web Audio.
