ORIGINAL PROCEDURAL SOUNDTRACK AND EFFECTS
Seven original score arrangements and fifteen synthesized effects are defined
in src/music.ts and src/audio.ts. Web Audio oscillators, deterministic noise,
filters, envelopes, an echo bus and a compressor generate all audio at runtime.
No third-party music, samples, audio files or audio service are used.
