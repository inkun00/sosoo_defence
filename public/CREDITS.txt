ORIGINAL PROCEDURAL SOUNDTRACK AND EFFECTS
Seven original score arrangements and local UI/collision effects are defined
in src/music.ts and src/audio.ts. Web Audio oscillators, deterministic noise,
filters, envelopes, an echo bus and a compressor generate those sounds locally.
Tower launches now use the twelve licensed samples credited below, with
procedural fallbacks when a sample is unavailable. No remote audio service.
